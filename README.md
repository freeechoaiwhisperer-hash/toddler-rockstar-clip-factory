# Toddler Rockstar Clip Factory

Zero-dollar pipeline for viral 30-second cartoon clips of a toddler as a rock star.

## Daily Automation
Grok fires at 9 AM Eastern every day with 5 fresh concepts + ready-to-paste prompts.

## Tools (free tiers)
- Dreamina (Seedance): 120 daily credits, image-to-video
- Kling 3.0: ~66 daily credits, best motion
- Pika: monthly credits, no watermark
- Google Flow (Veo Lite): limited daily
- CapCut: free editor, auto-captions, trending audio

## Workflow
1. Grab the 5 prompts from your notification.
2. Generate in 2 tools, keep the least melted version.
3. Stitch in CapCut: captions, zoom, hook text in first 2s.
4. Export 1080p vertical, no watermark.
5. Post 3-5x/day across TikTok, Reels, Shorts. Same clip, different captions.

## Character Consistency
Upload your son's photos to Dreamina or Kling as reference images so the cartoon toddler keeps his face/hair. Text-to-video alone will melt faces.
