# Master Prompt Library

## Core Style
Cute cartoon toddler rock star, exaggerated expressions, vertical 9:16, 5-15s motion, bright colors, no real faces, kid-safe chaos.

## Example Prompts
1. Diaper stage dive into a pile of stuffed animals, guitar in hand, crowd of toys cheering.
2. Toddler snacking on guitar picks like candy, headbanging, spit-take of pick crumbs.
3. Mosh pit of teddy bears and dinosaurs, toddler crowd-surfing on a toy car.
4. Mic scream fail: tiny mouth opens, soundwave knocks over the drum kit.
5. Air guitar solo that accidentally summons a cartoon lightning bolt.

## Variations
Rotate: food fights, instrument destruction, wardrobe malfunctions (diaper slip), encore demands.
